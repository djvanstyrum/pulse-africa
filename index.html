Pulse Africa helps dance schools across Africa turn their big online following into lasting support — more visibility, less admin, and more income for the young dancers they train.

Why we exist

Millions watch them dance. Few see the work behind it.

Many African dance schools have huge audiences online, run by small teams. The time goes to teaching and caring for students — and the opportunities that come with all that attention often slip by.

  • Attention doesn't turn into incomeViews and followers rarely lead to donations or sponsorships.
  • Too little timeAdmin and social media compete with teaching.
  • Messages go unansweredFans, brands and organisers reach out — replies are slow or never come.
  • Hard to findSchools without a viral video stay invisible to supporters.
What we do

Smart helpers for busy dance schools

We build simple AI helpers that take care of the routine work, so staff can spend their time on the dancers. A person at the school always stays in charge.

More visibility

Help planning and writing posts, so schools show up regularly and reach new supporters.

Less admin

Messages from fans, donors and partners sorted and answered faster — nothing important missed.

More income

Easy ways for fans to give, and help finding sponsors and partners beyond existing followers.

How it works

Start small, learn fast, then grow

We begin with one school in Uganda, build what they need most, and only grow once it truly works.

Now

Prepare

Listen to our first school, agree on what matters most, and put a child-safety policy in place.

Pilot

Build one or two helpers and test them for three months, measuring time saved and income earned.

Grow

Improve what works, add more helpers, and welcome more schools across Africa.

Children come first

Most dancers are children and young people. Our helpers never post photos or details of students on their own — a person at the school approves everything, and every partner school has a safeguarding policy before we start.
